## Plate builder rules
- Steps: 1 Letters (Standard, 3D Gel, 4D, 4D Gel, Bevelled) · 2 Plate (Standard, Short, Hex, Oversized, Motorcycle) · 3 Extras (Tint, Border) · 4 What do you need? (Full set / Front only / Rear only). A Front/Rear preview switch sits at the top.
- Rear plates are yellow, fronts white, no UK badge.
- Phone tilt: on touch phones the builder plate (.plate-stage.tilt) follows the phone's motion (deviceorientation, slowly re-centres to how it's held) and the letters' light (feDistantLight azimuth in the pf- filters) and a shine sweep move with it. iPhone shows a "📱 Tilt to look" pill to grant motion access; it disappears once motion arrives. Desktop keeps the hover tilt.
- Hex: no border. Motorcycle: Standard or 3D Gel letters only, no tint, rear only.
- Font: traced from the Charles Wright reference (79mm tall, 57mm wide, 14mm stroke, 11mm gaps, 33mm between groups; "1" is a plain bar). Glyph outlines are in `plate.js`; don't swap in a web font.
- Oversized = same width, taller plate, same letter size. Motorcycle = two lines with bigger letters.

- Road legal vs show plates: step 5 "Road legal or show plate?" (P.use). SHOW_ONLY in index.html = tint, hex, bevelled letters (forces Show plate; Road legal button disabled). Badge #useBadge under the options (green road legal / amber show plate) and the WhatsApp order says "Type: SHOW PLATES (not road legal)" or "Road-legal plates". 3D gel/4D/4D gel are treated as road legal (BS AU 145e); owner can change the lists.
- "See it on your car" (`carplate.js`, deferred; [data-carplate] button under the plate builder; needs window.PB from index.html): photo (camera or library) → finds the plate with the AI plate finder first (assets/ai/plate-finder-512.onnx = open-image-models YOLOv9-tiny 512, MIT licence file beside it, fetched by the manual workflow "Fetch plate AI model" into scratch branch fetched/plate-model; run on the phone with ONNX Runtime Web 1.31.1 wasm from jsDelivr, single thread, loaded in the background when the tool opens, 15s cap; output rows [b,x1,y1,x2,y2,cls,score]); the AI box is refined to exact corners by fitPlate() (plate-colour mask in the box, robust straight-line fits to the top/bottom/left/right edges, intersected, so angled plates follow their perspective), then detectAt(), else the trimmed box (S.boxOnly: no shape guess). Shape uses trueAspect() (perspective-corrected, assumed phone lens f=0.85×long side, kept through crops) and is only guessed (short <3.0, oversize text <0.58) when the plate is seen fairly straight on (yawed() = corner angles >9° off square, left/right edge ratio, or <90px wide → standard). Old black/silver plates don't set the light level. Tested on 28 real Wikimedia Commons car photos (manual workflow "Fetch test car photos" → scratch branch fetched/test-photos), and the tap mode prefers an AI box under the tap. If the AI can't load it falls back to the colour detector (yellow = rear, white = front blobs with dark text; convex outline; hex = rows near top/bottom shorter than the middle), no corner dragging (owner rule): if auto-detect fails, or on "Wrong spot? Tap your plate", the customer taps the plate and detectAt() flood-fills the plate colour from that spot (several seeds, so a tap inside a letter hole still works); "Crop photo" (white crop box with corner handles) crops then re-runs detection, classifies the current plate (standard / short / oversized by text-height ratio / hex / two-line) and recommends Oversized (Range Rover-style) or Hex if the chosen plate doesn't match. New plate = PLATE.render rasterised (defs inlined) and mesh-warped through a homography from the old plate's mm frame (centred), brightness, colour tone (per-channel white balance from the old plate) and grain matched to the photo, soft shadow. Old plate: Smart (paint out with pull-push fill + grain when the new plate is smaller or a different shape) / Paint out / Black backing / Car-colour backing / Leave. Views: Night (scene darkened, rear plate lit with plate lamps), Before/after slider, Close-up (tilting zoom with shine), Compare letters A/B. Image labels: "SHOW PLATE · Not for road use" or "ROAD LEGAL style · BS AU 145e" + SMR Plates pill. Download JPEG, Share link, Order on WhatsApp (includes type, current plate kind and share link). Photos can also be dragged in or pasted (Ctrl/⌘+V, or the "Paste a photo" button which uses navigator.clipboard.read on phones). Photos open via openPhoto() (createImageBitmap with resize, fallback Image) at max 1800 px, with an "Opening your photo…" overlay. Tested on synthetic photos only (sandbox has no internet).
- Share links (`share/`: api.php upload JPEG ≤4 MB, 20/hour per visitor, 400/day; index.php view-only page with og:image; img.php; lib.php): stored in private_dir()/shares for 30 days (cleanup on uploads). Pretty URL /m/<10-char id> (root .htaccess). Client helper window.SMRShare.upload(canvas, kind, title) in index.html; used by Mock-up Studio ("Share link" next to Download) and See it on your car.
