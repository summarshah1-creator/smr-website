About these numbers. Estimates for collection orders using published or commonly reported UK rates (Nov 2026). Setup and activation fees are not included. Uber Eats: 13% pickup fee + VAT. Just Eat: 14% + VAT commission + a 50p admin fee per order (as reported). Deliveroo: rates are negotiated per restaurant and not published; 14% + VAT is an estimate. FoodHub: 0% commission, 3.4% + 20p card fee per order, plus weekly rental (about £23 a week, around £100 a month on average). Rates are taken from each platform's own published pricing where it exists. If you've been given your own rate, tap "Use my own rates" to change it.

A month is worked out as 365 ÷ 12 days, a week as 7 days. Card fees apply wherever you take card payments, in-store too, so they aren't counted as commission.
