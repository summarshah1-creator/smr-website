Qibla compass

Find the qibla, wherever you are.

From Luton the Kaaba is 119° from north: roughly east-south-east. Hold your phone flat and turn until the Kaaba lines up with the top.

Keep away from metal and magnets, and move your phone in a figure of eight if it seems off.

Three ways listings arrive.

HomeInspire FM / About us
About Inspire FM

Four friends, one Ramadan, and a station that grew up with Luton.

Inspire FM began in 1997 as Radio Ramadhan Luton, started by four friends to give the town a spiritual connection during the holy month. It came back every Ramadan, growing as its message reached more people.

In 2010 the station went full-time on 105.1 FM as Inspire FM. Today it's a family of more than 150 volunteers, broadcasting from Dallow Road, running Inspire Eid, the Jummah Winter Appeal, the Big Iftar with partners, and supporting dozens of causes every year.

Our purpose hasn't changed: positively inspiring the community.
